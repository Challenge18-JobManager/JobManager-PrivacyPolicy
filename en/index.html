1. Who we are

Apply is a Mac app that helps you keep track of your job applications: jobs, stages, recruiter contacts, documents and events. It is developed by the Challenge 18 team.

This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored and for what purpose. The same text appears inside the app and on the app's App Store page.

2. Data you keep in the app

The app only handles what you type or add yourself:

  • Jobs: company, title, logo, link, platform, salary, work model, description, priority, stages, dates, result and feedback.
  • Recruiter contacts: the contact's name and link, tied to a job.
  • Documents: résumés, cover letters and other files you add, with their category and the jobs they belong to.
  • Profile: your "about me" text and your interview notes.
  • Notifications and preferences: stage reminders, the notification history and the app settings, such as the reminder time and where to store your data.

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other identifying data to work.

3. Where your data is stored

You choose one of two modes in Settings:

  • This Mac: your data stays in the app's folder on your Mac and never leaves it.
  • iCloud (Premium): your data is stored in your iCloud Drive, in your Apple Account, and syncs across your Macs. iCloud is an Apple service and follows Apple's Privacy Policy.

We have no servers and never receive a copy of your data, in either mode. The app does not send your data to the internet: only iCloud, when you choose it, and the App Store, for the subscription, use the network, through Apple's own services.

4. What your data is used for

Your data is only used to make the app work for you: showing your jobs and stages, organizing your documents, reminding you of next steps and creating events in your calendar when you ask it to.

We do not use your data for ads, behavioral profiles or any other purpose, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties.

5. Artificial intelligence

The app can read the text of a job post you paste and fill in the fields for you. This uses Apple's language model that runs on your Mac (Apple Intelligence). The text is not sent to any server, ours or anyone else's.

This feature only works on Macs with Apple Intelligence turned on. If it isn't available, you fill in the job by hand.

6. Calendar and notifications

Calendar

If you turn on Calendar sync, the app asks for access to your calendars to create and update events for your job stages. The app only changes events it created itself.

Events live in the calendar you use on your Mac. If that calendar belongs to an account such as iCloud, Google or Outlook, it syncs under that account's rules.

You can revoke access at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.

Notifications

Stage reminders are local notifications, created and delivered by your Mac. Nothing goes through a server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in System Settings › Notifications.

7. Premium subscription

The Premium subscription is purchased and managed by the App Store, with your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details, name or email.

The app only checks, on your Mac, whether there is an active subscription, to unlock Premium features. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the App Store, under Account › Subscriptions.

8. What we don't collect

  • There is no account, sign-up or login.
  • We do not collect usage, location or identifying data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

If you allow Apple, in your macOS settings, to share crash reports with app developers, we may receive those reports anonymously, through Apple, only to fix bugs. This depends on your consent and can be turned off in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

9. How to remove your data

Since your data stays with you, you remove it yourself, at any time:

  • In the app: delete the jobs, documents and notes you no longer want to keep.
  • On this Mac: when you remove the app, also delete its folder in ~/Library/Containers if it is still there.
  • In iCloud: delete the app's data in System Settings › [your name] › iCloud, under Manage storage.
  • In Calendar: events created by the app stay in your calendar until you delete them.

10. Your rights

Data protection laws such as Brazil's LGPD and the GDPR give you the right to access, correct and delete your data. In Apply you exercise these rights directly in the app, because we keep no copy. If you have questions, reach us at the contact below.

11. Children

The app is made for job seekers and is not directed at children. We do not collect data from anyone, of any age.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the text inside the app and on this page change together, along with the update date at the top. Important changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
